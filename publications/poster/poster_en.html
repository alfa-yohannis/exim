Building Your Own Local AI

Empower your organization's document search with AI

An independent case study on public export and import information from Indonesia's Directorate General of Customs and Excise (Bea Cukai) and Ministry of Finance. Ask any export or import question in everyday language; every answer cites the regulation or page it came from.

The problem

  • — Export and import rules are spread over thousands of documents across 18 ministries and agencies: regulations, procedures, permits, and service standards.
  • — Every product follows its own path, rules change without notice, and the partner country publishes in another language.

How it works

1
You type a question in everyday language.
2
The system searches about 2,000 documents copied from beacukai.go.id and jdih.kemenkeu.go.id and summarises the most relevant.
3
It shows evidence cards: the regulation or page, the quoted passage, and a link to the original on the official website.

What you get

What is import for domestic use, and which document releases the goods?
Import for domestic use is an import for use or ownership by a person or company domiciled in Indonesia, governed by PMK-190/PMK.04/2022 and PER-02/BC/20231. The goods are released with an import declaration (PIB) for each transport document, and the importer calculates the duties and taxes owed2.

Why you can trust the answers

  • ✓ Check it yourself. Every sentence carries a source number, and the original page opens right away.
  • ✓ No making things up. Answers come only from the indexed documents. If it isn't there, the system says so.
  • ✓ Dated copies. Every page is stored with its original address and the date it was fetched.
  • ✓ Data stays in the office. Everything runs on your own computer.

The technology behind it

The system uses RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation: the AI may only answer from the documents it finds. The AI model is interchangeable:

Local — Qwen3 8B on your own computer; questions and documents never leave the office.
Cloud — ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, when you want sharper answers.
