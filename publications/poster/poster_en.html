Building Your Own Local AI

Empower your organization's document search with AI

An independent case study on public information from Indonesia's Directorate General of Taxes (DJP). Ask any tax question in everyday language; every answer cites the regulation or page it came from.

The problem

  • — Tax rules are spread over thousands of pages on pajak.go.id: laws, regulations, articles, FAQs, forms, and their PDF attachments.
  • — One simple question can take half an hour of searching and reading one regulation after another.

How it works

1
You type a question in everyday language.
2
The system searches over 10,000 pages and regulations copied from pajak.go.id and summarises the most relevant.
3
It shows evidence cards: the regulation or page, the quoted passage, and a link to the original on pajak.go.id.

What you get

What are the personal income tax rates under the HPP Law?
Under the HPP Law (Law 7 of 2021), annual taxable income is taxed at 5% up to IDR 60 million, 15% up to IDR 250 million, 25% up to IDR 500 million, 30% up to IDR 5 billion, and 35% above that1. Withholding on employees' pay follows PP 58 of 20232.
1 UU 7 Tahun 2021 — Harmonisasi Peraturan Perpajakan — Peraturan · pajak.go.id
2 PP 58 Tahun 2023 — Tarif Pemotongan PPh Pasal 21 — Peraturan · pajak.go.id

Example answer. Each evidence card links to the original page on pajak.go.id.

Why you can trust the answers

  • ✓ Check it yourself. Every sentence carries a source number, and the original page opens right away.
  • ✓ No making things up. Answers come only from the indexed pages. If it isn't there, the system says so.
  • ✓ Dated copies. Every page is stored with its original address and the date it was fetched.
  • ✓ Data stays in the office. Everything runs on your own computer.

The technology behind it

The system uses RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation: the AI may only answer from the documents it finds. The AI model is interchangeable:

Local — Qwen3 8B on your own computer; questions and documents never leave the office.
Cloud — ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, when you want sharper answers.
